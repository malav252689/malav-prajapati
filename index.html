Malav Anup Prajapati

Mechanical Engineer specializing in UAV Design, Multiphase CFD, Industrial Automation, and Physics-Informed Machine Learning.

I am Malav Prajapati, a Mechanical Engineering undergraduate at Nirma University, pursuing a Minor Degree in Industrial Automation alongside my core studies. My academic and project interests lie at the intersection of Aerial Robotics, Computational Fluid Dynamics (CFD), Industrial Automation, and Physics-Informed Machine Learning.

My engineering journey revolves around designing and building high-performance UAVs, developing simulation-driven solutions, and applying automation principles to enhance system efficiency. I currently serve as the President of SAE Nirma Collegiate Club, I lead aeromodelling teams, guide UAV development, and oversee technical collaborations and events. I have contributed to multiple award-winning UAV projects at national and international competitions, and I am actively engaged in research involving Physics-Informed Neural Networks (PINNs), OpenFOAM-based thermal simulations, and data-driven modeling for multiphase flow problems.

With a strong foundation in automation systems, control logic, and machine-level integration, I bring together expertise from mechanical engineering, robotics, and computational sciences to build robust, efficient, and innovative engineering solutions. I am continuously exploring opportunities in aerospace engineering, simulation, industrial automation, and applied AI, aiming to develop technologies that combine precision, performance, and real-world impact.
