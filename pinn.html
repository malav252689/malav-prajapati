Physics-Informed Neural Networks

My research focuses on applying Physics-Informed Neural Networks (PINNs) to solve computational fluid dynamics and biomedical heat transfer problems.

The initial study investigated lid-driven cavity flow simulations using NVIDIA PhysicsNeMo, exploring architectures including FCNs, FourierNet, Modified FourierNet, and Highway FourierNet.

The work demonstrated strong capability of Fourier-enhanced PINNs in reproducing benchmark CFD solutions without conventional mesh-based numerical discretization.
