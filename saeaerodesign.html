SAE Aero Design West 2025 – Micro Class

SAE Aero Design West is an international competition organized by SAE International, featuring Micro, Regular, and Advanced Classes. The Micro Class presents one of the most constrained yet innovative UAV design challenges, requiring teams to create an aircraft that maximizes payload capacity while minimizing wingspan and weight.

Aircraft must be fully disassembled to fit inside a small volume, assembled quickly during a timed window, and complete the mission under a strict 450 W power limit. The payload consists of liquid water, testing the UAV’s structural efficiency and aerodynamic performance. Over 70 global teams competed in 2025.

Our aircraft was engineered for a 10-ft takeoff distance, requiring precise aerodynamic optimization, efficient propulsion matching, and lightweight composite structures. It successfully carried a 2-liter payload, achieving a high payload fraction — a key Micro Class performance metric.

Through advanced simulation, iterative prototyping, and rigorous testing, our team secured:
• 11th Overall Rank
• 5th Place in Mission Score among more than seventy international teams.
