SAE Aero Design West 2025 – Micro Class

SAE Aero Design West is an international competition organized by SAE International, featuring Micro, Regular, and Advanced Classes. The Micro Class, where our team competed, presents a unique engineering challenge centered around rapid assembly, compact packaging, and efficient payload transportation. Once airborne, the aircraft must complete mission tasks, meet aggressive weight constraints, and demonstrate stable flight within extremely tight operational conditions which includes a strict 450 W power limit and under 10 feet take-off distace for bonus points. The aircraft's payload consists of liquid water, testing the UAV’s structural efficiency and aerodynamic performance. The class evaluates creativity in compact structural design, manufacturability, lightweight engineering, and overall performance under constrained power and size.

In the 2025 season, our Micro Class aircraft was engineered to achieve a takeoff distance of just 10 feet, a requirement that demanded precise aerodynamic optimization, efficient propulsion matching, and minimal structural weight. Despite its compact size, the aircraft successfully carried a 2-liter water payload, achieving a payload fraction above 0.5, one of the key metrics of Micro Class scoring. This combination of rapid assembly, ultra-short takeoff capability, and high payload performance positioned our design as one of the strongest competitors at SAE Aero Design West 2025, contributing to our international ranking. Over 70 global teams competed in 2025.

Through advanced simulation, iterative prototyping, rigorous testing, and team work our team secured:
• 11th Overall Rank
• 5th Place in Mission Score
