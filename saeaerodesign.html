SAE Aero Design West 2025

SAE Aero Design West is an international aerospace competition organized by SAE International. Our team competed in the highly competitive Micro Class category focused on compact aircraft design, payload transportation, and rapid deployment.

The aircraft was engineered for ultra-short takeoff performance, lightweight structural optimization, and efficient aerodynamic behavior. The UAV successfully transported a 2-liter payload while maintaining a payload fraction above 0.5.

Competing against more than 70 international teams, the project demonstrated strong engineering integration, simulation-driven optimization, and advanced manufacturing execution.

Achievements

  • 11th Overall International Rank
  • 5th Place in Mission Score
  • International Finalist — SAE Aero Design West 2025
