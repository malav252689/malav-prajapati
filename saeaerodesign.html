SAE Aero Design West 2025 – Micro Class

SAE Aero Design West is an international competition organized by SAE International, featuring three categories – Micro, Regular, and Advanced. The Micro Class, where our team competed, presents one of the most constrained yet innovative design challenges. It challenges teams to design, build, and fly an unmanned aerial vehicle (UAV) capable of maximizing payload capacity while minimizing overall weight and wingspan. The payload, consisting of liquid water, tests both the structural efficiency and flight performance of the UAV. Aircraft in this category must be completely disassembled and stored within a small predefined volume, then assembled by the team in a short timed window before flight. Once airborne, the aircraft must complete mission tasks under a strict 450 W power limit, meet aggressive weight constraints, and demonstrate stable flight within extremely tight operational conditions. The class evaluates creativity in compact structural design, manufacturability, lightweight engineering, and overall performance under constrained power and size. Over 70 teams from universities across the globe participated in the 2025 edition.

In the 2025 season, our Micro Class aircraft was engineered to achieve a takeoff distance of just 10 feet, a requirement that demanded precise aerodynamic optimization, efficient propulsion matching, and minimal structural weight. Despite its compact size, the aircraft successfully carried a 2-liter water payload, achieving a payload fraction above 0.5—one of the key metrics of Micro Class scoring. This combination of rapid assembly, ultra-short takeoff capability, and high payload performance positioned our design as one of the strongest competitors at SAE Aero Design West 2025, contributing to our international ranking.

The project combined advanced simulation, iterative prototyping, and data-driven testing to enhance flight stability and control. These efforts paid off as the team secured an impressive 11th overall rank and 5th place in mission score among more than seventy international competitors.
